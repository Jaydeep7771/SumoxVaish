Our story

From dance partners to partners for life

We met as two teenagers, miles away from home, not knowing that one day, we’d find home in each other. What started as two Bhangra partners slowly became a love that grew up with us.

From graduations and first jobs to moving across the world and building a little life of our own, we’ve been by each other’s side through it all. Our first car, our first home, and all the little moments in between. We’ve watched each other grow, change, and become who we are today, finding a little more of ourselves in each other along the way.

We’re poles apart in so many ways, yet somehow, we fit together just right. In each other, we’ve found our safest place, our greatest comfort, and a love that makes even the most ordinary days feel special.

And through all these years, we’ve never stopped being dance partners. The music may have changed, but we’ve always found our way back to the same rhythm.

And now, those two teenagers are getting married!

As we begin this next chapter, we want nothing more than to be surrounded by the people we love, celebrating all that has brought us here and blessing all that lies ahead.

Here’s to a lifetime of growing together, finding home in each other, and never letting go of each other’s hand — on or off the dance floor.
